iLEAP Academy  Branding Guide 

BACKGROUND: 
The background should be the brand-specific White (#ffeefb) throughout the presentation to create a bright, modern, and open feel.

PRIMARY COLORS (DUAL): 
Use all these colors as the dominant themes for headlines, major shapes, and emphasis:
1.Red #C62828 
2.Blue #00296b 
3.green #008000 
4.Orange #e6690a 
5.Light Blue #0089ca 

LOGO PLACEMENT: 
Use the iLEAP Academy logo on the title slide and subtly in the footer corners on all subsequent slides.

Title can contain ONLY the title text, logo at the bottom and appropriate 4K image.

TYPOGRAPHY: 
Use large, readable fonts. 
- Headlines: Should be bold and use any of the primary colors.
- Body Text: Should be clear and legible, all other colors in variations

OVERALL TONE: 
Professional, positive, and friendly, targeting parents and students.

LANGUAGE: 
Keep it clear, simple, and inspiring.

DESIGN STYLE: 
Modern and clean with plenty of white space (do not clutter).

CONTENT FORMATTING: 
Avoid heavy bullet lists. Prefer short phrases, key points, and meaningful visuals and 4K Child image (inclusive different ethinicity).

IMAGERY: 
Include supportive visuals such as children learning, confidently, teamwork, and classroom engagement, with iLEAP Academy attires.
Add different ethnic groups children: white, Indian, Chinese, Black, Syrian

VIBE: 
Slides should feel energetic, hopeful, and future-focused
