What Students Build

A practical writing program for school success.

Students learn how to organize ideas, write stronger sentences and paragraphs, develop essays, use academic language, and revise their work with purpose.

1

Writing Structure

Students learn clear sentence structure, paragraph organization, introductions, body paragraphs, and conclusions.

2

Grammar & Vocabulary

3

Feedback & Revision

Three Writing Levels

Support that matches each student's stage.

Grade 2/3

Foundations of Great Writing

Build strong sentence structure, creative thinking, grammar confidence, vocabulary, and early paragraph writing in a fun and supportive environment.

Grade 4/5/6

Essay Writing Mastery

Develop paragraph and essay writing skills through organization, persuasive techniques, academic vocabulary, editing strategies, and critical thinking.

Grade 7/8/9

Advanced Essay & Academic Writing

Master thesis statements, persuasive argumentation, analytical thinking, evidence, academic vocabulary, and advanced revision techniques.
