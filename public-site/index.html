What Students Build

A practical writing program for school success.

The program focuses on the skills students need every week in school: organizing ideas, writing complete paragraphs, developing essays, editing clearly, and using stronger academic language.

1

Writing Structure

Students learn how to organize sentences, paragraphs, introductions, body paragraphs, and conclusions.

2

Grammar & Vocabulary

Lessons strengthen grammar, punctuation, transition words, word choice, and academic vocabulary.

3

Feedback & Revision

Students receive clear feedback so they can revise with purpose and improve over time.

Program Levels

Choose the right writing path.

Grade 2/3

Foundations of Great Writing

Build strong sentence structure, creative thinking, grammar confidence, vocabulary, and early paragraph writing in a supportive environment.

Grade 4/5/6

Essay Writing Mastery

Develop paragraph and essay writing skills through organization, persuasive techniques, academic vocabulary, editing strategies, and critical thinking.

Grade 7/8/9

Advanced Essay & Academic Writing

Master thesis statements, persuasive argumentation, analytical thinking, evidence, academic vocabulary, and advanced revision techniques.
