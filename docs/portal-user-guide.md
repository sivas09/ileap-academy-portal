# iLEAP Academy English Writing Portal User Guide

Portal: `https://english.ileapacademy.com/`

This guide explains how students, teachers, and administrators use the iLEAP Academy English Writing Portal.

## Quick Start

1. Open `https://english.ileapacademy.com/`.
2. Enter the email and password provided by iLEAP Academy.
3. Click `Sign in`.
4. Use the left sidebar to open the tools available to your account.
5. Click `Sign out` when finished, especially on a shared computer.

If your session expires, the portal will return you to the login screen and show:

```text
Your session expired. Please log in again.
```

Log in again to continue.

## Account Help

Use `Forgot password?` on the login page if you cannot remember your password.

Contact iLEAP Academy if:

- Your login does not work.
- Your account says it is waiting for approval.
- Your name, email, or writing level is wrong.
- You cannot see the correct resources or assignments.
- A paid resource does not unlock after payment.

## Portal Roles

| Role | Main Purpose | Common Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | View class materials, submit writing, and read feedback | Dashboard, Resources, Assignments, Feedback, Shop, Account |
| Teacher | Manage assigned level materials and review student writing | Dashboard, Resources, Assignments, AI Tutor, Review, Admin, Account |
| Admin | Operate the full portal | Dashboard, Resources, Assignments, AI Tutor, Review, Users, Website, Prompts, Admin, Account |

Your sidebar may look different depending on your role and account status.

## Students

Students use the portal to access writing materials, submit homework, and read feedback.

### Student Dashboard

After signing in, check that:

- Your name is correct.
- Your writing level is correct.
- Your assignments match your class.
- Your resources match your class.

If something is wrong, tell your teacher or iLEAP Academy.

### Resources

Open `Resources` to view class materials for your level.

Resources may include:

- PDFs
- Worksheets
- Lesson notes
- Books
- Video links

Click `Open resource` or `Download resource` to use a material.

Some resources may be locked because they are paid resources or have not been released yet.

### Assignments

Open `Assignments` to view writing homework.

Each assignment may include:

- Title
- Instructions
- Due date
- Recommended word count
- Topic or lesson
- Submission status

Read the instructions carefully before writing.

### Submit Homework

1. Open `Assignments`.
2. Find the correct assignment.
3. Click `Submit homework`.
4. Paste your paragraph or essay into the text box.
5. Check the word count.
6. Choose who to notify if the option appears.
7. Click `Submit`.

The portal accepts pasted writing text. It does not currently accept uploaded student homework files.

### Before Submitting

Review your writing for:

- Complete sentences
- Capital letters
- Punctuation
- Paragraph structure
- Clear examples
- Transition words
- Word count expectations

Do not submit blank text, unrelated text, or someone else's writing.

### Feedback

Open `Feedback` to read comments on submitted work.

You may see:

- AI feedback created by a teacher or admin
- Teacher feedback written manually
- Mark out of 10
- Grammar and punctuation notes
- Vocabulary suggestions
- Structure advice
- Overall improvement tips

Use feedback to revise and improve your writing.

### Shop

Open `Shop` to view optional books, bundles, or paid resources.

If you purchase a resource and it does not unlock automatically, contact iLEAP Academy.

### Student Rules

Students should not:

- Share passwords.
- Use another student's account.
- Submit another person's writing as their own.
- Try to access other students' work.
- Try to change portal settings.

## Teachers

Teachers use the portal to manage assigned writing levels, publish learning materials, review student writing, and provide feedback.

Teachers can work only with levels assigned by an admin. If a level is missing, ask an admin to update your teacher account.

### Teacher Dashboard

Use the dashboard to review:

- Assigned levels
- Recent assignments
- Recent submissions
- Available resources

### Manage Resources

Open `Resources` to view materials. Open `Admin` to add or manage materials.

Resources may include:

- Lesson PDFs
- Worksheets
- Books
- Writing examples
- Video links

To add a resource:

1. Open `Admin`.
2. Choose file upload or URL.
3. Enter a clear title and description.
4. Choose type, access mode, level, topic, and lesson.
5. Upload the file or paste the link.
6. Click `Add resource`.

Use clear names such as:

```text
Grade 7 Week 3 Persuasive Essay Worksheet
```

Do not publish unfinished materials. Save as hidden if the material still needs review.

### Create Assignments

Open `Assignments` or `Admin`.

1. Enter a short assignment title.
2. Add complete student instructions.
3. Add word count guidance if useful.
4. Choose due date if needed.
5. Choose level, topic, and lesson.
6. Publish when ready.

Teachers can create and manage assignments only for assigned levels.

### Edit Assignments

Open `Assignments`.

1. Find the assignment.
2. Click `Edit assignment`.
3. Update the title, instructions, due date, level, topic, lesson, publish status, or archive status.
4. Click `Save assignment`.

Archive assignments that should no longer be active but already have submissions. Delete only empty assignments.

### Review Student Submissions

Open `Review`.

1. Choose a level filter if needed.
2. Select a submission.
3. Read the student's writing.
4. Review any AI feedback.
5. Add manual teacher feedback.
6. Click `Save feedback`.

Manual teacher feedback is visible to the student.

### Use AI Tutor

Open `AI Tutor`.

1. Select the student.
2. Select the assignment or choose general writing practice.
3. Paste the student's writing.
4. Click `Get feedback`.
5. Review the AI feedback.

AI Tutor feedback may include:

- Mark out of 10
- Content
- Grammar and punctuation
- Academic vocabulary
- Structure
- Transition words
- Overall advice

Teachers should review AI feedback before relying on it. Add manual feedback when the AI misses class-specific instruction or needs correction.

### Good Feedback Practice

Helpful feedback should:

- Name one or two strengths.
- Explain the highest-priority correction.
- Give one clear next step.
- Use language appropriate for the student's grade level.
- Avoid overwhelming the student with too many corrections at once.

Example:

```text
Your topic sentence is clear, and your example supports the main idea. Next, fix sentence boundaries: sentence 3 is a run-on sentence. Break it into two shorter sentences so the reader can follow your point.
```

## Administrators

Admins manage the full portal: users, curriculum, resources, assignments, products, website text, and AI tutor settings.

Use admin accounts only for trusted staff.

### Admin Dashboard

Admins should regularly check:

- Student accounts are active and assigned to the correct level.
- Teacher accounts are assigned only to the levels they teach.
- Topics, lessons, resources, and assignments are published only when ready.
- Student submissions are being reviewed.
- Paid resources are connected to the right products.
- AI tutor prompts match iLEAP Academy feedback standards.

### Create A Student

1. Open `Users`.
2. In `Create User`, choose `Student`.
3. Enter first name, last name, email, and temporary password.
4. Choose the correct writing level.
5. Click `Create user`.
6. Give the student their email and temporary password.

Ask the student to confirm their dashboard shows the correct level after login.

### Approve A Pending Student

1. Open `Users`.
2. Find the student in the student list.
3. Review the account details.
4. Click `Approve`.

Pending students can access only limited account information until approved.

### Create A Teacher

1. Open `Users`.
2. In `Create User`, choose `Teacher`.
3. Enter teacher name, email, and temporary password.
4. Select every level the teacher should manage.
5. Click `Create user`.

Teachers can create and manage content only for assigned levels.

### Change User Access

Use `Users` to:

- Change a student level.
- Change a user role.
- Enable or disable an account.
- Reset a temporary password.
- Add or remove teacher level access.

Do not disable or demote the last active admin account.

### Manage Curriculum

Open `Admin`.

Use `Curriculum Topics` for broad units such as:

```text
Essay Structure
Persuasive Writing
Narrative Writing
```

Use `Lessons / Weeks` to create weekly lessons under a topic.

Recommended workflow:

1. Create the topic.
2. Add lessons or weeks under the topic.
3. Upload resources and attach them to the topic or lesson.
4. Create assignments and attach them to the topic or lesson.
5. Publish only the topic, lessons, resources, and assignments that students should see.

Students see only published curriculum for their assigned level.

### Bulk Upload Resources

Use `Bulk Curriculum Upload` when uploading many files for the same level, topic, or lesson.

1. Enter a description that applies to all files.
2. Choose resource type and access mode.
3. Choose level, topic, and lesson.
4. Decide whether files should publish immediately.
5. Select all files.
6. Click `Upload files`.

File names become resource titles, so rename files clearly before upload.

### Manage Shop Products

Open `Admin`, then use `Create Product` or `Manage Products`.

For each product:

1. Enter product title and description.
2. Choose individual or bundle.
3. Set price.
4. Assign the level if applicable.
5. Select included resources.
6. Mark active when ready for students.

If Stripe is enabled, students can purchase active products in the portal.

### Manage AI Prompts

Open `Prompts`.

Use prompts to control how AI feedback is written.

Before saving a new active prompt:

- Include the iLEAP writing rubric.
- Ask for clear explanations of mistakes.
- Keep the tone appropriate for children.
- Test with sample writing.

Saving a prompt creates a new active version. Older prompts stay in history.

### Manage Website Text

Open `Website`.

Admins can update:

- Landing page headline
- Subtitle
- Announcement
- Login helper text
- Grade card titles and descriptions

Keep website text concise and parent-facing.

## Weekly Workflows

### Student Weekly Workflow

1. Sign in before class or homework time.
2. Open `Resources` and review lesson materials.
3. Open `Assignments`.
4. Complete the writing task.
5. Submit your writing.
6. Return later to read feedback.
7. Revise your writing using the feedback.

### Teacher Weekly Workflow

1. Confirm the week's topic and lesson are published.
2. Confirm resources are visible.
3. Publish the assignment.
4. Monitor submissions in `Review`.
5. Run AI Tutor where useful.
6. Add manual feedback.
7. Ask students to read feedback and revise.

### Admin Weekly Checklist

- Confirm new students are approved.
- Confirm teacher level access is correct.
- Publish this week's topics, lessons, resources, and assignments.
- Hide old drafts or unfinished content.
- Check that submitted homework appears in `Review`.
- Confirm paid products point to the correct resources.
- Confirm password reset email and submission notifications are working.

## Security And Privacy

All users should:

- Keep passwords private.
- Sign out after using a shared computer.
- Use only their own account.
- Report suspicious account activity to iLEAP Academy.

Teachers and admins should:

- Avoid downloading student work to shared computers.
- Avoid sharing student feedback outside the correct class context.
- Use strong passwords.
- Disable accounts that should no longer have access.
