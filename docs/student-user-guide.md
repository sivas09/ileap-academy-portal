# iLEAP Academy Portal Student Guide

Portal: `https://english.ileapacademy.com/`

This guide is for students using the iLEAP Academy English Writing Portal to view class materials, submit writing, and read feedback.

## Sign In

1. Open `https://english.ileapacademy.com/`.
2. Enter the email and password provided by iLEAP Academy.
3. Click `Sign in`.

If you cannot sign in, use `Forgot password?` or ask iLEAP Academy for help.

## Pending Approval

If your account says it is waiting for approval, iLEAP Academy still needs to verify your registration. You will not see assignments, resources, shop materials, or feedback tools until your account is approved.

## Dashboard

After signing in, the dashboard shows your portal overview.

Check that:

- Your name is correct.
- Your writing level is correct.
- Your assignments and resources match your class.

If your level is wrong, tell your teacher or iLEAP Academy.

## Resources

Open `Resources` to see class materials for your level.

Resources may include:

- PDFs
- Worksheets
- Lesson notes
- Books
- Video links

Click `Open resource` or `Download resource` to use the material.

Some resources may be locked because they require purchase or have not been released yet.

## Assignments

Open `Assignments` to see writing homework.

Each assignment may show:

- Title
- Level
- Instructions
- Due date
- Recommended word count
- Topic or lesson
- Submission status

Read the instructions carefully before writing.

## Submit Homework

1. Open `Assignments`.
2. Find the assignment.
3. Click `Submit homework`.
4. Paste your paragraph or essay into the text box.
5. Check the word count.
6. Choose who to notify if the option appears.
7. Click `Submit`.

The portal currently accepts pasted writing text, not uploaded student files.

## Before You Submit

Review your writing for:

- Complete sentences
- Capital letters
- Punctuation
- Paragraph structure
- Clear examples
- Transition words
- Word count expectations

Do not submit a blank answer or unrelated text.

## Feedback

Open `Feedback` to read feedback on submitted work.

You may see:

- AI feedback created by a teacher or admin
- Teacher feedback written manually
- Mark out of 10
- Grammar and punctuation notes
- Vocabulary suggestions
- Structure advice
- Overall improvement tips

Use the feedback to revise your writing.

## Shop

Open `Shop` to view optional books, bundles, or paid resources.

If you purchase a resource through the portal, it should unlock automatically after payment. If a paid resource does not unlock, contact iLEAP Academy.

## Change Password

Open `Account` to change your password.

Use a password you can remember but do not share it with classmates.

## Student Rules

Students should not:

- Share passwords.
- Use another student's account.
- Submit another person's writing as their own.
- Change portal settings.
- Try to access other students' feedback or submissions.

## Weekly Student Workflow

1. Sign in before class or homework time.
2. Open `Resources` and review the lesson materials.
3. Open `Assignments`.
4. Complete the writing task.
5. Submit your writing.
6. Return later to read feedback.
7. Revise your writing using the feedback.
